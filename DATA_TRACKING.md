## 📂 Data Requirements by Category

### 1. 🏛️ Officials
*   **Requirements:** Names, titles, office locations, and official contact details.
*   **Notes:** Focus on the Mayor, Vice Mayor, and City Council members first. 

### 2. 💰 Budget
*   **Requirements:** Latest annual budget summary and available expenditure reports.
*   **Notes:** Ensure figures are cited from official local government releases.

### 3. 🚧 Projects
*   **Requirements:** Name of the project, status (Ongoing/Completed), budget allocation, and target beneficiaries.
*   **Notes:** Stick to major infrastructure and social programs to start.

### 4. ⚖️ Ordinances
*   **Requirements:** Title, ordinance number, date passed, and a brief summary.
*   **Data Source:** Import and verify data from [Ley de Zamboanga](https://zamboangacity-ordinance.vercel.app/).
*   **Notes:** Verify that the schema matches our Astro MDX frontmatter requirements.

### 5. 📞 Contact Information
*   **Requirements:** Department names, phone numbers (hotlines), official email addresses, and verified social media pages.
*   **Notes:** High priority for emergency response units (CDRRMO, Police, Fire).
