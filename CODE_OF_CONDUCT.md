## Our Pledge

Better Zamboanga City is an independent civic-tech project dedicated to serving the community of Zamboanga City. We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of technical experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected Behavior:**

- Demonstrate empathy, respect, and consideration in all interactions.
- Use welcoming, constructive, and inclusive language.
- Accept and deliver constructive technical feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what best serves the citizens of Zamboanga City and the open-source community.
- Prioritize strict factual accuracy and integrity when contributing civic data.

**Unacceptable Behavior:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal discrimination in any form.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal/political attacks.
- Publishing private, non-public personal information without consent (doxxing).
- Submitting fabricated, unverified, or misleading government records.
- Utilizing the platform or repository for partisan political campaigning, propaganda, or commercial advertising.
- Any conduct detrimental to a transparent, public-interest civic project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository (pull requests, issues, discussions, and commit messages).
- Associated communication channels, including contributor group chats and social channels.
- Any public setting where an individual represents Better Zamboanga City.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainer via the contact methods listed on their GitHub profile or via direct message.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, maintaining confidentiality for the reporter.

**Enforcement Actions:**

1. **Correction:** A private written warning providing clarity on the violation.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with consequences for continued violations.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary ban from interacting with the repository and project communication spaces.
4. **Permanent Removal:** A permanent ban from all repository spaces, issues, and associated communication groups.
